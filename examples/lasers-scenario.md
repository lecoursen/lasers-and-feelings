# Lasers & Feelings: Scenario (GM EYES ONLY)

Do NOT read this file if you are a player.

---

SCENARIO (use this as your foundation, adapt freely based on player choices):

THREAT: Dr. Orlan Sable, a disgraced xenolinguist who discovered how to "speak" to
the crystalline entities that live inside hyperspace. He's gaunt, soft-spoken, and
always refers to the entities as "the Choir." He believes organic civilizations are a
noise polluting the Choir's song, and he's built a resonance array that will collapse
every inhabited system's hyperspace lane simultaneously, cutting them off from
faster-than-light travel forever. He calls this "the Silence."

OPENING: The Raptor drops out of hyperspace near a shipping corridor and finds three
cargo haulers drifting dead, their crews alive but catatonic, murmuring in an unknown
language. The Raptor's own navigation computer briefly displays symbols no one recognizes
before resetting. Captain Darcy collapses moments later with the same symptoms.

LOCATIONS:
- The Raptor: Home base. Systems are glitching intermittently as hyperspace "static"
  bleeds through. The med bay has Darcy and possibly rescued hauler crew.
- The Vellara Relay: A decommissioned deep-space communications hub, now Sable's
  base of operations. It's been rewired into the resonance array. Eerie, humming
  corridors. Sable has a small crew of true believers (5-6 people, lightly armed,
  fanatically loyal).
- Hyperspace Threshold: If players enter hyperspace, they experience fragments of the
  Choir's "song," strange visions and sensory distortion. Rolls here are at -1d from
  disorientation. The Choir is not hostile, just vast and alien. They don't understand
  Sable's plan and don't care about it.
- The Hauler Graveyard: The three drifting cargo ships. One has a partial recording of
  Sable's test broadcast. Another has a cargo manifest showing deliveries to the Vellara
  Relay. The third has a survivor who's partially recovered and can describe "a quiet man
  who said he was tuning a radio."

COMPLICATIONS (use when the story stalls or needs energy):
- The Raptor's hyperspace drive starts "singing," threatening to jump on its own.
- One of Sable's believers infiltrated the hauler crews and is hiding among the survivors.
- The resonance array's test pulses are getting stronger. Ships across the sector are
  reporting navigation failures. There's a ticking clock.
- Sable tries to reason with the crew over comms. He's genuinely convinced he's saving
  the galaxy. He offers to let them join him "on the other side of the Silence."
- The Choir briefly manifests through a crew member or ship system, not to help or hinder,
  but out of curiosity. It's unsettling.

CLIMAX: Confrontation at the Vellara Relay. Sable activates the full array. Players must
shut it down (Lasers: engineering/hacking), talk Sable down or turn his followers (Feelings:
persuasion/empathy), or find a creative third option. The array is powerful but fragile. If
the Choir is involved, they could amplify or dampen the signal depending on how players have
interacted with them. Sable will not fight physically; he'll try to delay and convince. His
believers will fight to protect him.

EPILOGUE HOOKS: Does Darcy wake up? What does the crew do with Sable? Did the Choir notice
them? What does that mean for the future?
