# Lasers & Feelings: Crew of the Raptor

## Ship

- **Strengths:** Superior Sensors, Cloaking Device
- **Problem:** Only One Medical Pod (and Captain Darcy is in it)

## Characters

- 4LI3N (4L), Android Communications Engineer, Number 5, Goal: Fulfill contract with the Vestigial Court of Gorgon 6
- Dr. Vesper Scalpel, Dangerous Doctor, Number 3, Goal: Prove Yourself
- Kade "Overload" Morrow, Dangerous Engineer, Number 4, Goal: Keep Being Awesome

---

## Backstories

### 4LI3N (4L)

4LI3N is a Nexius Horizon v4.20 android contracted to translate and provide technical support for Judge Axl Denton of the Vestigial Court of Gorgon 6 on a diplomatic mission. While subconsciously performing a diagnostic defragmentation, the ship was commandeered, Judge Denton was taken prisoner, and 4L was tossed out an airlock. Left to float and observe the Milky Way, 4L cross-referenced sensor observations with the DHEEP (Database of Human Emotional Experience in Practice v9) and found more than several matches. It was beautiful.

The Raptor responded to 4L's distress beacon. While indebted to the crew and willing to support them, 4L is obligated to notify everyone that the contract with the Vestigial Court of Gorgon 6 does not list piracy as a material nor anticipatory breach, and therefore does not qualify for contractual rescission. 4L still has a job to do.

### Dr. Vesper Scalpel

Dr. Vesper Scalpel once held the most prestigious surgical posting in the Consortium: Chief of Xenobiological Trauma at Arcturus Prime Medical Center, where she saved more lives than any doctor in recorded history. Her hands were legendary, her instincts almost supernatural. But Vesper never played by the rules. She'd throw out the treatment protocols, override the diagnostic AIs, and operate on pure gut feeling, often while the rest of her surgical team watched in horror. The Consortium Medical Board tried to rein her in dozens of times, but it's hard to discipline a doctor whose patients keep surviving procedures that every textbook says should be fatal.

Then came the Kelaris Incident. Vesper made a split-second call during a mass casualty event that saved an entire colony, but her methods were so wildly unorthodox that the resulting inquiry couldn't be swept under the rug. Rather than strip her credentials (they couldn't afford to lose her skills entirely), the Board quietly reassigned her to the Raptor as its field medic: far from any hospital, any oversight committee, and any press corps. Now, with Captain Darcy comatose in the ship's only medical pod (a patient she is dying to crack open and treat her own way), Vesper is stuck patching up the crew with whatever's on hand and proving that medicine isn't about following the manual. It's about having the guts to do what needs to be done.

### Kade "Overload" Morrow

Overload earned the nickname after rerouting the Raptor's entire plasma grid through an unauthorized bypass during a Zeltron ambush. A move that should have vaporized the engine room but instead tripled weapon output for exactly eleven seconds. That was enough. Since then, Kade has a reputation: every fix is a gamble, every modification is a miracle waiting to happen, and safety protocols are "suggestions written by people who've never been shot at." The crew has learned to trust the smell of burning insulation coming from his workspace.

He knows he's riding a streak. Every impossible hack that works, every jury-rigged system that holds together with spit and audacity, raises the bar for next time. He can't go back to being ordinary, not after the crew started calling him a genius, not after Captain Darcy stopped questioning his "creative interpretations" of engineering manuals. So he keeps pushing, keeps improvising, keeps being the most dangerously brilliant wrench-turner in the sector. The day he plays it safe is the day he becomes forgettable, and that scares him more than any reactor meltdown.
